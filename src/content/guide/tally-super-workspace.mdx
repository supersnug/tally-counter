# Arrange the Tally workspace

The workspace editor opens a side toolbox while leaving Tally's editable surfaces visible. Drag toolbox items to their intended destination rather than creating a detached preview panel.

## Text elements

Add text in Tally's normal Manrope style or its alternative DM Mono style. Set the text and color before placing it, then move, resize, scale, or rotate it after placement.

Permanent top- and bottom-bar text cannot be removed, but supported text can be repositioned or copied where the editor permits.

## Statistics

Place full-size or mini versions of Session Actions, Net Movement, Total Distance, Most Active, Active Counters, Goals Complete, Increments, Decrements, and Resets.

These are live statistics, not text imitations. Their values and visual treatment match the corresponding Stats interface.

## Counters and surfaces

Counters can remain in the standard grid or be placed individually on supported workspace surfaces. Settings and Stats can receive custom content, while Settings itself remains available so the app cannot become unconfigurable.

## Toolbox workflow

Click a toolbox item to expand its preview and choose text, color, or size before placing it. Drag the configured element from the toolbox to the surface where you want it.

## Editing placed elements

Select a placed item to move, rotate, resize, or independently scale it. Use Remove when you no longer want that customized item.
