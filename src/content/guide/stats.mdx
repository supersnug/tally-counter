# Understand activity statistics

Open Stats from the top bar to inspect activity across your counters.

## Workspace statistics

Stats include Session Actions, Net Movement, Total Distance, Most Active, Active Counters, and Goals Complete.

Net Movement measures the signed overall change. Total Distance counts movement regardless of direction, so adding `5` and subtracting `5` produces zero net movement but ten units of distance.

## Action statistics

Increments, Decrements, and Resets report specific interactions. These can also appear as full or mini Tally Super elements.

## Reset one statistic

Hover a statistic to see “Click to reset,” then select it to clear only that value.

## Reset everything

Use Reset all stats at the bottom of the menu when you want a completely fresh activity history.

<Warning>Resetting statistics does not change counter values, but the cleared activity history cannot be reconstructed automatically.</Warning>
