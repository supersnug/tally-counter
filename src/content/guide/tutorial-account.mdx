# Sign in and resolve your first sync

This simulation demonstrates the account flow without contacting Supabase. The demonstration credentials are fixed and read-only. Simulate sign-in, then choose how to resolve the first-device conflict.

<LiveAccountLesson />

## What happened

Tally found both local and cloud counters and asked which collection should become synchronized. It did not silently overwrite the counters already on the device.

## What a real account adds

- Synchronization between devices
- Counter-copy sharing and shared groups
- Recovery of supported settings, scripts, and Tally Super data

<Important>Real counters continue to be saved locally while you are signed in. Signing out does not remove them from the current device.</Important>
