# Rebuild Tally around the way you count

Tally Super is Tally's visual customization system. It does not merely change colors or card sizes: it lets you rearrange workspace content and redesign the elements inside individual counters while preserving their real behavior.

## Two customization areas

**Workspace customization** changes the broader Tally interface. Add text and statistics, position live counters, and place supported content on the main page or inside Settings and Stats surfaces.

**Counter customization** changes one counter. Move its title, value, buttons, goal bar, indicators, and quick settings independently without changing other counters.

## Start an editor

Open **App Settings → Tally Super** to start the workspace editor. To redesign one counter, open that counter's settings, choose **Tally Super**, and enter its full-screen editor.

Both editors use direct manipulation: select an element, drag it on its surface, then use transform controls for precise position, scale, dimensions, and rotation.

## What Super does not change

Transforms do not alter a counter's value, steps, limits, or goals. Core data remains usable even if customization is removed. Resetting or deleting a customization restores the normal Tally layout rather than deleting the counter.
