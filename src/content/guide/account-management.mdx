# Update or remove your account

Open the account panel from the top bar, then use the settings cog for account and sharing preferences.

## Change your username

Your username identifies you for copy sharing and group invitations. Usernames must remain unique, so a name already used by another account cannot be saved.

## Change password or email

Password changes may require a reauthentication code when the session is no longer recent. Email changes are available while signed in and require confirmation through the new email address.

## Sign out

Signing out stops cloud synchronization on that device. Counters already saved in the browser remain available, including counters marked local.

## Delete account

Account deletion removes the Supabase account and its cloud-owned data. It does not remove personal counters stored locally in the browser.

<Critical>Account deletion cannot restore cloud data later. Export an All backup first if you may need to move that data to another account or device.</Critical>
