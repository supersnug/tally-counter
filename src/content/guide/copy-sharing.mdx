# Send an independent counter copy

Open the send action on a personal counter and enter the recipient's username or email address.

## Optional linked data

Choose whether to include the script and Tally Super customization linked to that counter. The recipient may accept the counter while declining either optional addition.

## Recipient choices

The recipient can accept, decline, and choose whether the imported counter should be local. Accepting creates an independent personal counter; later changes do not travel back to the sender.

## Outcome notifications

The sender receives a message when the copy is accepted, declined, or blocked because the recipient has disabled incoming copies. Acknowledging the result clears that completed share request.

<Important>Sending a copy does not grant access to the original counter.</Important>
