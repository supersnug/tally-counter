# Values, steps, goals, and limits

Every counter has independent positive and negative step amounts. Values can cross zero in either direction, and optional minimum and maximum values act as hard boundaries.

## Starting and exact values

The **starting value** is used by Reset. **Exact value** changes the current count without changing that reset point. Jump to a saved value provides shortcuts to your start, limits, and goals.

## Multiple directional goals

Choose **More than** when progress moves upward or **Less than** when it moves downward. Tally orders milestones in the direction of travel and displays them as slices in a smooth progress bar.

Reaching the final goal marks the counter complete, but counting can continue. Only minimum and maximum limits stop the value.

<Tip>Use goals for milestones you may exceed. Use a minimum or maximum only when the counter must stop at that value.</Tip>

## Trash and recovery

Deleted counters enter Trash for five days by default. They remain editable and embeddable until restored or permanently deleted.
