# Add an account only when you need one

Tally works without an account. Signing in adds cross-device synchronization, usernames, counter-copy sharing, shared groups, and cloud backups of supported preferences.

## What remains local

Personal counters are still saved in the browser while signed in. Local counters never enter your personal cloud data. Signing out or deleting an account does not erase counters already stored on the device.

## What synchronizes

Non-local personal counters, scripts, supported preferences, and Tally Super data synchronize to your account. Shared counters use their group's shared storage instead of personal counter data.

<Important>An account adds features; it is not required to keep using Tally on the current device.</Important>
