# Use your first counter

A counter begins at its starting value. The large buttons apply its independently configured positive and negative steps, while Reset returns to the start.

<LiveCounterLesson />

## What you just used

- **+** added the positive step of `1`.
- **−** subtracted the negative step, stopping at the minimum of `0`.
- The progress bar moved through goals at `5` and `10`.
- Reset returned the value to its configured start.

A counter's Settings menu contains its name, steps, limits, goals, direction, and color. This playground keeps those values fixed so the lesson can focus on counting.

<Tip>Set the starting value to the number you want Reset to restore. Changing the exact value does not change that reset point.</Tip>
