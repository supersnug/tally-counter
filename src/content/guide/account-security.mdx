# Sign in and recover access

Create an account with an email address, username, password, and matching confirmation password.

## Password requirements

Passwords need at least eight characters, including a lowercase letter, uppercase letter, digit, and symbol. Tally displays each requirement while you type.

## Username or email login

After account creation, the sign-in field accepts either your username or email address. Usernames also let other users find you for copies and group invitations without needing your email.

## Email confirmation

Supabase sends account and security messages through the configured email provider. Tally accepts the confirmation or recovery token in the app rather than requiring a separate confirmation page.

## Password recovery

Use Forgot password while signed out to request a recovery code. Signed-in users can change their password from Account Settings. When secure password change requires recent authentication, Tally asks for a reauthentication token.

<Warning>Never share confirmation, recovery, or reauthentication tokens. They are intended only for the account action you requested.</Warning>
