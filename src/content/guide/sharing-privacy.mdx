# Control how other users can reach you

Open Account Settings → Sharing to configure copy and group preferences.

## Anonymize shares

When enabled, recipients see “A Tally user” instead of your username on copy-sharing messages.

## Sending PIN

Protect outgoing counter copies with a six-digit PIN. The PIN is required when sending, not when receiving.

## Receive counter copies

Turn this off to block incoming copies. You may still send copies to users who accept them. Senders receive a specific unavailable message rather than a normal decline.

## Receive group invitations

Disable this separately when you do not want new group invitations. Existing group membership is not removed.

<Important>Copy controls and group-invitation controls are independent. Turning off one does not disable the other.</Important>
