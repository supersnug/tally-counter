# Count together with shared groups

Create and manage groups from the Groups tab in Account Settings. Invite signed-in users by username or email.

## Invitations

Recipients see a Join or Decline prompt. Users who disable group invitations cannot receive new requests.

## Shared Counters tab

After joining a group, Shared Counters appears beside My Counters. If you belong to multiple groups, use the group selector; with one group, the selector stays out of the way.

## Shared storage

Group counters, linked scripts, and linked customizations remain in the group. They are not copied into each member's personal counters or browser backup data.

## Leaving and deletion

Owners manage members and may delete the group. Members can be removed without changing personal counters. Deleting a group removes its shared counters for everyone.

<Warning>Deleting a group affects every member. Export anything that should survive as a separate personal counter before deleting it.</Warning>
