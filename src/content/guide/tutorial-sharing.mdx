# Choose between copies and groups

The two tabs demonstrate the actual copy form and the Group section of Account Settings. All recipients and members are simulated.

<LiveSharingLesson />

## Send a copy

Use the paper-plane button on the sample counter. The production copy modal accepts a username or email and independently includes the linked script and Super customization. Submitting it produces a local success message only.

## Start a group

Switch to Group to inspect group selection, members, permission access, invitation controls, and group deletion. The fields are fixed demonstration data and do not contact Supabase.

<Tip>Start with Counting Only, Settings Only, Scripts Only, Super Only, or Full Access. Use Custom only when a member needs a precise combination.</Tip>
