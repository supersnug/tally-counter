# See how direction changes progress

Goals are not always positive. Switch between **More than** and **Less than**, then count in the matching direction. Notice that the milestone order and progress movement reverse together.

<LiveGoalLesson />

## The important distinction

A goal is a milestone and may be exceeded. A minimum or maximum is a hard limit and cannot be exceeded. The example uses limits only to keep the playground within a useful range.

<Tip>Use Less than for progress that moves downward, even when some of the goals are positive. Direction—not the sign of a goal—controls milestone order.</Tip>
