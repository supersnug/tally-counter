# Shape the everyday Tally experience

Open Settings from the top bar. App Settings is organized into areas such as Customization, Backup, account-aware preferences, and Tally Super.

## Customization

Change how counters are arranged and sized, how prominent their values appear, which visual details are shown, and how deletion behaves.

## Backup

Export or import Counters, Tally Super, or All. Counter operations open a secondary options modal when scripts or per-counter customization can be included.

## Tally Super

Use the Tally Super area for broader workspace customization and entry into its visual editor.
