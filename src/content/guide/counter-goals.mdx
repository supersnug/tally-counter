# Build a path with multiple goals

Goals are milestones rather than limits. A counter may pass a goal, continue through later goals, and exceed the final goal.

## More than

Choose More than when progress generally moves upward. Goals are ordered from lowest to highest, and each is complete when the count reaches or exceeds it.

## Less than

Choose Less than when progress moves downward. Goals are ordered from highest to lowest, and each is complete when the count reaches or falls below it.

<Important>The sign of a goal does not decide direction. A Less than counter may contain positive goals, and a More than counter may contain negative goals.</Important>

## Progress slices

Each goal creates a slice in the progress line. When one action crosses several goals, Tally moves through those slices smoothly in order. The displayed percentage describes progress toward the next goal, while additional progress details are available from its tooltip.

## Completion

The counter reports Complete after reaching its final goal. Completion does not lock the count; use a maximum or minimum when the value must stop.
