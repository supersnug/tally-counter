# Give each member the right level of access

Group owners assign permissions when inviting a member and can change them later.

## Presets

- **Full Access** allows every counter action except deleting the group itself.
- **Settings Only** allows counting, reset, and normal counter settings without scripts or Tally Super.
- **Scripts Only** allows TallyScript and JavaScript editing.
- **Super Only** allows counter name, color, and Tally Super customization.
- **Counting Only** allows add, subtract, and reset.

## Custom access

Custom permissions separate counting actions, individual settings, each scripting language, counter deletion, and individual Tally Super elements.

<Tip>Use a preset whenever possible. Custom permissions are best for a specific exception, such as allowing TallyScript but not JavaScript.</Tip>
